Our story

A fresh approach to mentoring

WeMentors Academy was founded on a simple frustration: crowded classrooms and one-size-fits-all coaching leave too many students behind. We started tutoring out of a shared belief — every student learns differently, and deserves to be taught that way.

We're building a mentoring practice spanning Grades 3–10 across CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE and IB, plus a Spoken English & Public Speaking track for students, professionals and homemakers alike. From day one, our batch sizes stay small — small enough that your mentor knows exactly where you're stuck.

Our mission

To give every student, wherever they live, access to a mentor who knows them by name — not a seat number.

Our vision

A world where personalized mentoring isn't a luxury reserved for a few, but the default way students learn.

Student-first, always

Every decision, from batch size to session length, is made by asking what actually helps the student in front of us.

Teaching philosophy

Concepts first, memorization never

We teach the “why” behind every formula and rule, because concepts that are understood outlast concepts that are memorized. Every mentor is trained to adapt pace and explanation style to the individual student — not the other way around.

Digital whiteboard classrooms

Live, interactive sessions with real-time annotation and screen sharing.

Small batch sizes

1-on-1 or up to 8 students per batch, so mentors can actually mentor.

Curriculum-mapped material

Study material and mock tests aligned to each board's syllabus and pace.
