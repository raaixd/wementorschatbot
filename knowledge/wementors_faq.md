# WeMentors Knowledge Base

## Organization Overview

### What is WeMentors?

WeMentors Academy provides personalized mentoring designed around
each learner’s needs, pace, strengths, and learning gaps.

### Who can use WeMentors?

WeMentors supports students in Grades 3–10, as well as students,
professionals, and homemakers interested in Spoken English and
Public Speaking.

allowing learners to receive individual attention.

## Mentoring Process

### How does the mentoring process work?

The mentoring process has four stages:

1. Diagnose: A short assessment identifies the student’s strengths
   and learning gaps.
2. Chart the path: Mentors create a learning plan based on the
   student’s board, pace, and goals.
3. Guide, live: Students attend interactive live sessions using
   digital whiteboard tools.
4. Review and adjust: Progress is reviewed with parents weekly,
   and the plan is adjusted when needed.

### Are progress updates provided?

Yes. The website states that progress is tracked and shared with
parents every week.

### Are doubt-solving sessions available?

Yes. WeMentors provides dedicated doubt-solving sessions.
Some programs also include fortnightly doubt clinics or priority
doubt-clearing access.

### What teaching approach does WeMentors use?

WeMentors focuses on understanding concepts rather than memorizing
answers. Lessons are connected to practical applications, and
mentors adapt their pace and explanation style to each learner.

## Programs

### What is the Foundation Years program?

The Foundation Years program supports Grades 3–5 and includes:

- Mathematics
- Science
- English
- Environmental Studies
- All core subjects

It focuses on strong fundamentals, curiosity-first teaching,
concept games, visual learning, and weekly progress notes for parents.

### What is the Middle School program?

The Middle School program supports Grades 6–8 and includes:

- All subjects
- Doubt solving
- Practical labs

It focuses on concept-based learning, practical problem sets,
fortnightly doubt clinics, and progress dashboard access.

### What is the Senior School Focus program?

The Senior School Focus program supports Grades 9–10 and includes:

- Mathematics
- Science
- Board preparation

It focuses on concept clarity, board-exam preparation, weekly mock
tests with review, and priority doubt-clearing access.

### What is the Confident Speaker program?

The Confident Speaker program is designed for students,
professionals, and homemakers. It includes:

- Spoken English
- Public Speaking
- Interview Skills

It uses a conversation-first approach and emphasizes practical
communication rather than rote grammar drills.
