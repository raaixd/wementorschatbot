## General information

### What is WeMentors?

WeMentors Academy provides personalized mentoring designed around
each learner’s needs, pace, strengths, and learning gaps.

### Who can use WeMentors?

WeMentors supports students in Grades 3–10, as well as students,
professionals, and homemakers interested in Spoken English and
Public Speaking.

## Grades and eligibility

### Which grades are supported?

- Grades 3–5: Foundation Years
- Grades 6–8: Middle School
- Grades 9–10: Senior School Focus

### Which curricula are supported?

- CBSE

- IB

## Programs

Listed in website order (used for “first / second program” follow-ups):

1. Foundation Years (Grades 3–5)
2. Middle School (Grades 6–8)
3. Senior School Focus (Grades 9–10)
4. Confident Speaker

### What is the Foundation Years program?

Supports Grades 3–5. Includes Mathematics, Science, English,
Environmental Studies, and all core subjects. Focus: strong fundamentals,
curiosity-first teaching, concept games, visual learning, and weekly
progress notes for parents.

### What is the Middle School program?

Supports Grades 6–8. Includes all subjects, doubt solving, and practical
labs. Focus: concept-based learning, practical problem sets, fortnightly
doubt clinics, and progress dashboard access.

### What is the Senior School Focus program?

Supports Grades 9–10. Includes Mathematics, Science, and board
preparation. Focus: concept clarity, board-exam preparation, weekly mock
tests with review, and priority doubt-clearing access.

### What is the Confident Speaker program?

Designed for students, professionals, and homemakers. Includes Spoken
English, Public Speaking, and Interview Skills. Conversation-first
approach; practical communication rather than rote grammar drills.

## Admissions

### Is a demo class available?

Yes. WeMentors advertises a free 30-minute demo class with no obligation
to continue.

### What happens during the demo class?

Visitors can meet a mentor, complete a short diagnostic related to the
relevant subject, and receive a personalized learning-plan recommendation.

## Teaching approach

### What teaching approach does WeMentors use?

WeMentors focuses on understanding concepts rather than memorizing
answers. Lessons are connected to practical applications, and mentors
adapt their pace and explanation style to each learner.

### Are classes online or offline?

Classes are conducted live and online through video calls with
digital whiteboard support.

### Are one-on-one classes available?

Yes. WeMentors offers one-on-one mentoring as well as small batches.

### What is the batch size?

Batches are typically one-on-one or capped at up to eight students.

## Mentorship

### How does the mentoring process work?

1. Diagnose: a short assessment identifies strengths and learning gaps.
2. Chart the path: mentors create a learning plan based on board, pace, and goals.
3. Guide, live: interactive live sessions using digital whiteboard tools.
4. Review and adjust: weekly parent progress reviews; the plan is adjusted when needed.

### Are progress updates provided?

Yes. Progress is tracked and shared with parents every week.

### Are doubt-solving sessions available?

Yes. Dedicated doubt-solving sessions are provided. Some programs also
include fortnightly doubt clinics or priority doubt-clearing access.
